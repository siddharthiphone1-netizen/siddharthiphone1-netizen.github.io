Clip Studio

Clip Studio is a private, self-hosted tool that the owner of the OnlineGaming YouTube channel uses to turn long gaming streams into short vertical clips, review them, and publish the approved ones to that channel.

What it does

  • Finds the strongest moments in a stream, cuts them into vertical clips and adds captions.
  • Lets the channel owner review every clip and approve or reject it.
  • Uploads approved clips to the owner’s own YouTube channel at scheduled times and adds them to the right playlist, using the YouTube Data API.

Who can use it

Clip Studio runs on the owner’s private server and is not offered to the public. The only YouTube account it connects to is the owner’s own channel.

Clip Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. See also our privacy policy and terms of service.
